## What is Elula?

Elula brings planning, reviews and releases into one workspace. It connects to the tools your team already uses and keeps a live view of every project.

## Core concepts

- **Workspaces** hold your team, projects and integrations.
- **Projects** group the work behind a goal or release.
- **Workflows** automate the steps between merge and release.

  New to Elula? The quickstart takes about five minutes.
</Callout>
