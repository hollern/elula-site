<Callout variant="note" title="Who can do this">
  By default, the **Administrator**, **Manager**, and **Procurement Manager** roles can create, edit, and finalize purchase orders.
</Callout>

A purchase order records what you're buying from a vendor, how much, and at what cost. You save it as a draft first, which you can keep editing. When it's correct, you finalize it. That locks it, and you can then receive items against it.

## Create a purchase order

1. In the sidebar, choose **Purchasing** › **Purchase Orders**.
2. Choose **New Purchase Order**.
3. Under **Order Details**, choose the **Vendor**.
4. Optionally, fill in the other order details (see [Purchase order details](#purchase-order-details)).
5. Under **Line Items**, fill in the first row:
   1. Choose the **Item**.
   2. Enter the **Qty**.
   3. Choose the **Unit**.
   4. Enter the **Unit Cost**.

   The **Total** column shows Qty × Unit Cost.
6. To add more items, choose **Add Item**. To remove a row, choose its trash icon.
7. Optionally, add notes under **Memos & Notes**.
8. Choose **Save Draft**.

Elula shows **Purchase order created** and opens the new order with the status **Draft**. Elula gives the order a **PO Number** when you save it.

### Purchase order details

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Vendor** | Who you're ordering from. Required. You can't change it after you save. |
| **Currency** | The currency of the order. Defaults to USD. |
| **Vendor Reference** | The vendor's own reference for this order, such as their quote number. |
| **Expected Delivery** | When you expect the delivery. |
| **Location** | The location the order is for. Receipts for this order are recorded at this location. You can't change it after you save. |
| **Internal Memo** | Notes for your team only. |
| **External Memo** | Notes for the vendor, included on the document. |
| **External Note** | A short note for the document footer. |

### If the order won't save

- **Vendor is required**: choose a vendor.
- **Add at least one line item before saving.**: add an item to at least one row. Elula ignores rows that have no item and a unit cost of 0.
- **Failed to create purchase order. Please try again.**: check that every row has an **Item** and a **Unit**, and a **Qty** greater than 0.

## Edit a draft purchase order

You can edit a purchase order only while it's a **Draft**.

1. Open the purchase order.
2. Choose **Edit**.
3. Make your changes. You can change the **Currency**, **Vendor Reference**, and **Expected Delivery**, the quantities, units, and costs of existing line items, and the notes on the **Notes** tab. To remove a line, choose its trash icon.
4. Choose **Save**.

Elula shows **Purchase order saved**.

## Finalize a purchase order

Finalize the order when its details and line items are final.

1. Open the draft purchase order.
2. Choose **Finalize**.
3. In the **Finalize purchase order?** dialog, choose **Finalize**.

Elula shows **Purchase order finalized**, and the status changes to **Approved**.

> [!WARNING]
> Once you finalize a purchase order, it's locked and you can't edit it. Check the line items before you finalize.

After you finalize the order, you can [receive items](/docs/purchasing/receive-items-on-a-purchase-order) against it.

## Purchase order FAQ

### Why can't I see my new purchase order in the list?

The **Purchase Orders** list opens on the **Open Purchase Orders** view, which doesn't show drafts. To see your draft, switch to the **All Purchase Orders** view.
