<Callout variant="note" title="Who can do this">
  By default, the **Administrator**, **Manager**, and **Procurement Manager** roles can add and edit vendor contacts.
</Callout>

A vendor can have several contacts, such as an account manager and someone in accounts receivable. You'll find them on the vendor's **Contacts** tab.

## Add a contact

1. In the sidebar, choose **Purchasing** › **Vendors**, then open the vendor.
2. Choose the **Contacts** tab.
3. Choose **New Vendor Contact**.
4. Enter the contact's **Full Name**. Optionally, add their **Role** (for example, "Account Manager"), **Email**, and **Phone Number**.
5. Choose **Create**.

Elula shows **Vendor contact created** and adds the contact to the list. If you leave **Full Name** empty, the dialog shows **Full Name is required**.

## Edit a contact or make it the primary contact

1. On the vendor's **Contacts** tab, choose the contact's name.
2. Choose **Edit**.
3. Update the details. To mark this person as the vendor's main contact, set **Primary Contact** to **Yes**.
4. Choose **Save**.

Elula shows **Contact saved**. In the **Contacts** list, primary contacts have a **Primary** badge.
