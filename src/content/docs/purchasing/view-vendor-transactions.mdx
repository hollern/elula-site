<Callout variant="note" title="Who can do this">
  By default, the **Administrator**, **Manager**, **Procurement Manager**, **Accountant**, **Sales Manager**, and **Viewer** roles can see a vendor's transactions.
</Callout>

Each vendor's page keeps a running history of your dealings with them. Use it to check what's on order, what's arrived, and what you've been billed and paid.

## View a vendor's transactions

1. In the sidebar, choose **Purchasing** › **Vendors**, then open the vendor.
2. Choose the **Transactions** tab.

The tab lists the vendor's purchase orders, purchase receipts, vendor invoices, and payments, with each one's **Reference**, **Date**, **Amount**, and **Status**. To open a transaction, choose its **Reference**.

## List a vendor's purchase orders

1. Open the vendor and choose the **Related Records** tab.
2. Choose **Purchase Orders**.

A list of the vendor's purchase orders opens. Choose a **PO Number** to open that order.
