<Callout variant="note" title="Who can do this">
  By default, the **Administrator**, **Manager**, **Procurement Manager**, and **Warehouse Manager** roles can receive items.
</Callout>

When a delivery arrives, record it against its purchase order. You don't have to receive everything at once. Record each delivery as it arrives, and Elula keeps track of what's still outstanding.

Before you start, make sure the purchase order is **Approved**. You can only receive items on a purchase order after it's been [finalized](/docs/purchasing/create-a-purchase-order#finalize-a-purchase-order).

## Receive items

1. In the sidebar, choose **Purchasing** › **Purchase Orders**, then open the purchase order.
2. Choose the **Receiving** tab.
3. Choose **Receive Inventory**.
4. For each line, enter the quantity that arrived in **To Receive**. It starts at the quantity still outstanding.
   - If part of a line arrived, lower the number.
   - If none of a line arrived, enter 0.
5. Choose **Save Receipt**.

Elula shows **Purchase receipt created**. The receipt appears in the **Purchase Receipts** list on the **Receiving** tab with the status **Complete**. The **Received** and **Remaining** columns on the order's **Line Items** tab update to match.

### What the columns mean

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Ordered** | The quantity on the purchase order. |
| **Received** | The quantity already received on earlier receipts. |
| **Remaining** | What's still outstanding: **Ordered** minus **Received**. |
| **To Receive** | The quantity arriving in this delivery. |

### If the receipt won't save

- **Enter a quantity to receive for at least one item.**: enter a quantity greater than 0 on at least one line.
- An error that starts with "Purchase orders can only be received from approved status": the purchase order is still a draft. [Finalize it](/docs/purchasing/create-a-purchase-order#finalize-a-purchase-order) first.
- **You don't have permission to perform this action.**: your role can't receive items. Ask your administrator.

## View a purchase receipt

1. On the purchase order's **Receiving** tab, choose the **Receipt #**.
2. The receipt opens, showing its **Receipt Details** and the items and quantities you received on the **Line Items** tab.

To attach files to the receipt, such as the vendor's delivery paperwork, use its **Documents** tab.

## Receiving FAQ

### Can I receive more than I ordered?

No. **To Receive** can't be higher than **Remaining**. If you type a larger number, Elula changes it to the remaining quantity.

### Can I receive a delivery in two parts?

Yes. Save a receipt for what arrived now. When the rest arrives, choose **Receive Inventory** again. Each delivery gets its own receipt in the **Purchase Receipts** list.
