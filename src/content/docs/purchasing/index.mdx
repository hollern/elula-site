Purchasing covers everything you buy from outside suppliers. You keep a list of vendors, place purchase orders with them, record deliveries as they arrive, and track the vendor's invoices until they're paid.

A typical purchase moves through these steps:

1. [Add the vendor](/docs/purchasing/add-a-vendor) you're buying from.
2. [Create a purchase order](/docs/purchasing/create-a-purchase-order) listing the items, quantities, and costs, then finalize it.
3. [Receive the items](/docs/purchasing/receive-items-on-a-purchase-order) when the delivery arrives.
4. [Enter the vendor's invoice](/docs/purchasing/enter-a-vendor-invoice).
5. [Record your payment](/docs/purchasing/record-a-vendor-payment) against the invoice.

## Purchasing terms

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Vendor** | A business you buy from. Each vendor has its own contacts and a history of transactions. |
| **Purchase order** | An order you place with a vendor: what you're buying, how much, and at what cost. It starts as **Draft**. When you **Finalize** it, it becomes **Approved** and can no longer be edited. |
| **Purchase receipt** | A record of items you received against a purchase order. Each delivery gets its own receipt, so one order can have several. |
| **Vendor invoice** | A bill from a vendor. You can link it to the purchase order it's for. |
| **Payment** | A payment you made against a vendor invoice. Recording payments moves the invoice from **Open** to **Partially Paid** or **Paid**. |
| **Vendor group** | A category of vendors, such as **Ingredient Suppliers** or **Packaging Suppliers**. You can see your organization's vendor groups under **Settings** › **Classifications** › **Groups** › **Vendor Groups**. |

## Find your way around purchasing

1. In the sidebar, expand **Purchasing**. It contains **Vendors**, **Purchase Orders**, and **Vendor Invoices**.
2. Choose one to open its list. Choose a name or number in the first column to open that record.
3. From a list, choose the **New** button (for example, **New Purchase Order**) to create a record.
4. To jump straight to a page, open **Search** in the sidebar (or press ⌘K on a Mac, Ctrl+K on Windows) and start typing, for example "Purchase Orders".

## Who can use purchasing

What you can do depends on your role. These are the default roles. Your organization's administrator may have changed them.

| Role | View vendors and purchase orders | Add vendors and contacts | Create and finalize purchase orders | Receive items | Enter vendor invoices and payments |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Administrator | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Manager | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Procurement Manager | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Warehouse Manager | Yes | No | No | Yes | No |
| Accountant | Yes | No | No | No | Yes |
| Head Brewer | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Sales Manager | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Viewer | Yes | No | No | No | No |

If you need to do something your role doesn't allow, ask your administrator.
