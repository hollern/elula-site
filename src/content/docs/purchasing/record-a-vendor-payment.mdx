<Callout variant="note" title="Who can do this">
  By default, the **Administrator**, **Manager**, and **Accountant** roles can record vendor payments.
</Callout>

After you pay a vendor, record the payment on their invoice. You can pay an invoice in full or in parts, and Elula updates the invoice's status as payments add up.

## Record a payment

1. In the sidebar, choose **Purchasing** › **Vendor Invoices**, then open the invoice. Its status must be **Open** or **Partial**.
2. Choose **Mark as Paid**.
3. Check the **Amount**. It starts at the invoice's full **Grand Total**. If you're paying part of the invoice, enter the amount you paid.
4. Optionally, change the **Payment Date** (it starts at today) and enter the **Payment Method** and **Payment Reference**, such as a check or transaction number.
5. Choose **Mark as Paid**.

Elula shows **Payment recorded**. If your payments now add up to the **Grand Total**, the invoice's status changes to **Paid**. Otherwise it changes to **Partial**.

> [!WARNING]
> The **Amount** always starts at the full **Grand Total**, even when part of the invoice is already paid. For a second or later payment, enter only the amount you're paying now. Otherwise you'll record more than you owe.

## See an invoice's payments

Open the invoice and choose the **Payments** tab. It lists each payment's **Payment #**, **Payment Date**, **Method**, **Reference**, and **Amount**.

## Vendor payment FAQ

### Can I change a payment after I record it?

No. A recorded payment can't be edited. Check the amount and date before you choose **Mark as Paid**.
