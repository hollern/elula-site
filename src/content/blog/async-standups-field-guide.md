For a year, our team replaced the daily standup meeting with a written update. Here's what worked, what didn't and what we'd do differently.

## Why async

We work across six time zones. A daily meeting meant someone was always joining early or late, and most updates didn't need a conversation.

## The format

1. What did you ship yesterday?
2. What are you working on today?
3. What's blocking you?

## What we learned

Blockers got resolved faster because they were written down, and the updates doubled as a searchable record of the team's work.
