Workflows 2.0 is available today on every plan. It's the biggest update to workflows since launch, shaped by hundreds of conversations with teams like yours.

## Conditional steps

Steps can now run only when a condition is met, such as a label on the pull request or a change to a specific folder.

```yaml title=".elula/workflows/release.yml"
steps:
  - name: Deploy docs
    if: changed("docs/**")
```

## Approvals

Require sign-off from a person or team before a workflow continues.

> [!NOTE]
> Approvals are available on the Team and Enterprise plans. See [pricing](/pricing).

## Visual editor

Build and edit workflows on a canvas instead of in YAML. Your existing workflows open in the editor automatically.
